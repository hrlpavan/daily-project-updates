### 3. Core Banking Authentication Gateway Discovery:

- Successfully intercepted and documented the live authentication microservice on `prime.plata.co`:
  - **OTP Dispatch**: `POST /auth/api/v1/auth-flow/otp/send` (`200 OK`, supporting `whatsapp`, `sms`, `voice-call`).

- Formulated the high-impact attack hypothesis targeting **Account Takeover / Missing Rate Limiting** on the 10,000-combination PIN space ($3,000 – $5,000+ bounty tier).

### 1. GitLab Community Contribution #2: Omnibus GitLab (MR !9851)
- **Target Issue**: [GitLab Issue omnibus-gitlab#841](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-org/omnibus-gitlab/-/work_items/841)
- **Upstream Merge Request**: [GitLab MR !9851](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-org/omnibus-gitlab/-/merge_requests/9851)
- **Target Repository**: `gitlab-org/omnibus-gitlab` (Branch: `master`)
- **Community Source Remote**: `gitlab-community/gitlab-org/omnibus-gitlab` (Branch: `hrlpavan-doc-mattermost-docker-ssl`)
- **Impact & Scope**:
  - Investigated the root cause of `x509: certificate signed by unknown authority` errors when integrating standalone Mattermost with GitLab running inside a Docker container.
  - Mattermost is written in Go; its runtime `crypto/tls` validates certificates against the container's operating system certificate store (`/etc/ssl/certs/ca-certificates.crt`), not GitLab's internal OpenSSL bundle.
  - Documented complete troubleshooting procedures in [`doc/settings/ssl/ssl_troubleshooting.md`](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-org/omnibus-gitlab/-/blob/master/doc/settings/ssl/ssl_troubleshooting.md):
    1. Inspecting container logs with `docker logs gitlab`.
    2. Injecting certificates into `/usr/local/share/ca-certificates/` and running `update-ca-certificates`.
    3. Adding certificates to `/etc/gitlab/trusted-certs/` and running `gitlab-ctl reconfigure` & `gitlab-ctl restart`.
  - Enforced full compliance with the GitLab Documentation Style Guide (`doc/AGENTS.md`).

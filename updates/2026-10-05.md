### 2. GitLab Contributor Platform Leveling & Points Architecture
Deep analysis of the GitLab Contributor Success program (`contributors.gitlab.com`) to formulate an optimized leveling roadmap:

#### Points Scoring Matrix
- **Merge Request Created**: `+20 pts`
- **Commit Merged**: `+20 pts` per commit
- **Merge Request Merged**: `+60 pts`
- **Issue Linked to MR (`Closes #...`)**: `+30 pts`
- **Community Bonus Label (`community-bonus::100`)**: `+100 pts` bonus

#### Contributor Tiers & Milestone Thresholds
1. **Level 1 (25 pts)**: Unlocks Level 1 profile badge & 5 Contributor Success store credits.
2. **Level 2 (500 pts)**: Unlocks Level 2 profile badge & 50 Contributor Success store credits.
3. **Level 3 (2,500 pts)**: Unlocks Level 3 profile badge & 150 Contributor Success store credits.
4. **Level 4 (7,500 pts)**: Unlocks Level 4 profile badge & 300 Contributor Success store credits.
5. **Level 5 (37,500+ pts)**: Unlocks Level 5 profile badge & 600 Contributor Success store credits.
6. **Core Team (Election)**: Requires consistent, high-impact contributions; grants **Developer permissions on GitLab projects**, **GitLab Ultimate personal license**, and private Slack access.

**Current Progression**: The 3 active MRs already in review represent **~490 points**, which will immediately clear **Level 1** and place the profile within **10 points of Level 2**.
